Frequently asked questions

Does it cost our business anything?

No, there's no cost to your business. We provide the machine and manage everything.

Who stocks and maintains the machine?

We do. We restock, service and maintain it — you don't need to do anything.

Can we choose what goes in?

Yes. We'll suggest a starting range, but we can tailor it to your team and adjust it over time.

How does the charity bit work?

We offer the option to donate 10% of the profits from your machine to a local charity you choose.

What happens if there's a problem with the machine?

Just contact us. We're local and will sort it quickly.

How do we get started?

Send us a quick message with your details and rough staff numbers, and we'll take it from there.

Could a machine work in your workplace?
